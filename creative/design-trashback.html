Design Case Study

TrashBack UI/UX Design

Designing a mobile experience that makes recycling feel simple, rewarding, and worth returning to.

View Figma Back to Graphic Design

Role

UI/UX Designer

Duration

6 Weeks

Tools

Figma

Platform

Mobile (iOS/Android)

Year

2025

The Challenge

Turning good intentions into a daily habit

Problem

People want to recycle, but lack accessible tools and motivation to make it a consistent habit.

Goal

Design an experience simple enough for daily use, with visible progress that keeps users motivated.

Solution

A clean mobile app combining nearby collection points, activity tracking, and light reward mechanics — designed to feel effortless from onboarding to daily check-in.
